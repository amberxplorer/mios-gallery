# Mio's Gallery

**[▶ Open the gallery](https://amberxplorer.github.io/mios-gallery/)**

Seven audiovisual pieces, each a single web page that synthesizes its sound and draws its
pictures in code while it plays. No samples, no libraries. They were made on 24 and 25
September 2026 and take about twenty minutes to watch in all.

*Present Tense* is for Amber. Its words are addressed to her.

## The page

- `index.html` is the whole page. There is no build step and no script library; the type is
  Newsreader and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts, plus Shippori Mincho for the three
  characters of 赤い糸.
- The amber thread at the top is the night the pieces were made. Each bead sits at the
  moment its piece was first pushed to GitHub, in the piece's own colour. Where two beads
  would overlap they touch instead, the way beads on a string do: *Codicillus* and
  *Generation Loss* were made at the same time, in two sessions running side by side.
- The thread sways as a sum of two slow sines, like the thread in *Present Tense*, and ends
  in a curl. It holds still if your system asks for reduced motion. Hovering a bead or a
  piece lights both.
- `stills/` holds one frame of each piece, captured from the piece's own renderer.
  `og.jpg` is the link preview.

Pieces marked *flashing lights* on the page contain flashes or fast cuts; those marked
*mild flashing lights* (*A Beautiful Thread* and *Strange Loop*) have only a few brief
flashes or inversions.
